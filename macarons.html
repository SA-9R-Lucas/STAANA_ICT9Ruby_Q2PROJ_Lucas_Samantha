Macarons!

    Steps

  1. Mix the dry ingredients into a bowl
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg whites into a foamy texture (May use a manual, hand mixer or stand mixer)
  3. Mix the wet ingredients into a another bowl
  4. Gradually add the dry ingredients (preferably use a strainer to make sure the dry ingredients are thoroughly mixed)
  5. Add the flavor of choice
  6. Mix ,but do not over mix, till the mixture is thick and foamy
  7. Pour the mixture into a piping bag and pipe the desired size onto a baking sheet
  8. Rest the piped batter on the baking sheet for 15 minutes before putting in the oven
  9. Set in the oven for 15 minutes at 325 degree fahrenhei
  10. To check if fully cooked lightly press on the surface for softness and hardness. Its cooked when you flip it and it comes off clean from the baking sheet.
  11. Match the sizes before piping the custardd in between

    Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup of egg white
  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1 cup of white sugar
  • (optional)1/2 meringue powder
  • 1 tsp of baking soda
  • 1/2 Tbsp of salt
  • 1 cup of powdered sugar

Custard Filling

Despite being able to add everything as a filling, heres the traditional custard filling of Macarons!

    Steps

  1. Mix the ingredients into a bowl
  2. Whip till consistency is thick and bubbly
  3. Add the pinch of Meringue powder
  4. Pour into the piping bag then pipe onto the macarons
  5. Press down very lightly and rest in room temperature for 15 minutes to solidify
  6. Transfer now into the fridge to finally set it, keep it there for only 5 minutes

    Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup of egg white
  • 1/4 cup of white sugar
  • pinch of meringue powder
  • 1/5 cup of condensed milk
  • 1/2 Tsp of salt
  • 1 cup of powdered sugar
  • 1/4 cup of all purpose cream

Now sit back and enjoy your home made Macarons!
